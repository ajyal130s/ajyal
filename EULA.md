### 5.2 Data Transmitted to the Publisher's Licensing Server
When you activate, sign in, or the Software performs a periodic license check, the following data is transmitted to and stored on the Publisher's licensing server:

* Google account identifier (Firebase UID)
* Email address associated with the signed-in Google account
* Display name and, if provided by the identity provider, profile picture URL

### 5.3 Google Authentication
Sign-in is handled through Google OAuth. By signing in, you also agree to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy. The Publisher does not receive your Google password.

### 5.4 Update Checks
The Software periodically contacts GitHub (`api.github.com`) to check for new releases. This transmits your IP address and a standard user-agent string. No personal data is transmitted during update checks.

### 5.5 Cloud AI Processing (Optional, Opt-In)
The Software offers an optional Cloud AI feature that you must explicitly enable and consent to before each session. When enabled, and only after your in-app confirmation, the current image you are processing is transmitted over the internet to the third-party AI provider **Replicate**, along with any API key you supply.

* You are solely responsible for the images you choose to send and for ensuring you have the right to send them.
* The Publisher has no control over how third-party providers process, retain, or disclose the data you transmit to them.
* **Important:** Do not enable Cloud AI for confidential, sensitive, medical, legal, or otherwise protected content unless you have independently verified that the provider's terms permit such use.
* The Publisher disclaims all liability arising from your use of third-party AI services.

### 5.6 Scanner Integration
The Software can interface with locally attached scanners via the operating system's native scanning APIs (WIA on Windows, SANE on Linux/macOS). Scanned images are processed locally and are not transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly enable Cloud AI or export them yourself.
